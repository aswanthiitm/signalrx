The discovery

Third-party plant (Pharmathen) behind the North America decline

Cipla's sharpest North America decline came from a plant it does not own: Pharmathen, the exclusive maker of lanreotide (~15% of US sales in FY25, broker estimate), closed an FDA inspection with observations on 21 Nov 2025; North America revenue then fell 28% quarter-on-quarter in Q3FY26 ($233M → $167M), a quarter that ended 14 days before the company disclosed the problem, and has stayed at $155M–$167M a quarter since — with FY27 guidance now assuming zero lanreotide, the $1.0bn exit run-rate target needs 54% more than the latest quarter.

Why it matters

  • Third-party plant risk sits outside the company's own site list and FDA status: it appears first in the partner's inspection record, which is why a dashboard built on the company's filings misses it.
  • Timing: the inspection closed 21 Nov 2025; the hit landed inside the Q3FY26 quarter; the company disclosed on 14 Jan 2026 (54 days after the inspection) and mainstream coverage followed on 16 Jan 2026.
  • Sizing: a full loss of lanreotide (~$34M a quarter) explains about 51% of the Q3FY26 step-down; a second driver accounts for the rest (see counterargument).
  • Escalation went the wrong way after the inspection: 21 Nov 2025: FDA inspection at Pharmathen, Rodopi → 7 Jan 2026: Redacted Form 483 becomes public → 13 Mar 2026: Class II recall of lanreotide syringes.
  • Management has stopped counting on a fix: "FY27 guidance includes no lanreotide contribution." — recovery now depends entirely on new launches.

Strongest counterargument

A second driver hit North America: US lenalidomide volume limits end: unlimited generic entry (31 Jan 2026). It lands after the Q3FY26 quarter closed, so it explains the failure to recover in later quarters rather than the step itself. A full loss of lanreotide (~$34M/quarter) explains about 51% of the Q3FY26 step-down; the remaining 49% needs another explanation (earlier erosion of the second driver, base-business price erosion). Also: A headline already carries the core terms: "Cipla warns of temporary lanreotide supply disruption after USFDA observations at supplier.". Management's position: "Compare the decline sequentially, not YoY (high base)." "Target a $1 billion North America exit run-rate."
